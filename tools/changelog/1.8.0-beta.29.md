- **Menus as smooth as your monitor**: Etterna ran the themes' moving parts only 60 times a second, whatever the frame rate, so the pointer, the song list, the logo's visualizer and the beat in the menus moved at 60 per second even on a 200 Hz monitor. - **The beat**: the logo and the song select's play button now pulse right on the music's beat (they were up to a sixtieth of a second late).
- **Fixed: shapes that got smaller could leave bits of their old shape drawn** (the logo's drifting triangles, some panels).
