- **Combo skin:** the combo in any skin's digits (osu! or Quaver skins), picked with a preview, in all four looks.
- **Quaver's custom judgement windows:** Quaver's dialog with Peaceful to Impossible and your own (create, name, sliders). Etterna judges on them; Wife% stays J4; the accuracy shown is Quaver's.

- **Results:** the big accuracy is on the timing you played with (a fail shows the accuracy of what was played), J4 small, other judges in the tooltip.
