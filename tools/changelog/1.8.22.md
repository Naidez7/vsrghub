2026-10-25
- **Colours in Customize gameplay**: a new Colours tab. Any part of the noteskin in a colour of yours: notes (all, or column by column), LN heads, bodies and ends, receptors (and pressed), the stage background, the stage hint, hit and hold lighting, mines. A full picker: a saturation / value square, a hue strip, an alpha strip, R / G / B / A sliders, a hex field to type into, and a palette of stock and recent colours. Undo (or Ctrl+Z) steps back one change at a time; "Skin's own" puts the skin's art back for that part alone; Reset this tab clears them all.
- The part is drawn from a grey copy of its art tinted in your colour, so a purple note asked for in red comes out red, not brown. The launcher makes those copies in the background the first time it starts after this update (a few seconds; until a copy exists the original is tinted instead).
- Colours are kept per noteskin and key mode, and go with the skin settings you export.
