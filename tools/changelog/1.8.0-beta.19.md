- **osu!, Quaver and Qwilight are found on any drive.** osu! (stable) is looked for where Windows says osu!.exe is (osu!'s installer registers it), in its usual folder, and in an osu! folder at the top of any drive, in Games or in Program Files. Quaver and Qwilight are looked for in every Steam library, with Steam itself found wherever it was installed. If one is still missed, `vsrghub.exe --osu "<folder>"` (or `--quaver`, `--qwilight`) points to it, and the log says when only osu!lazer is there (its maps can't be read).
