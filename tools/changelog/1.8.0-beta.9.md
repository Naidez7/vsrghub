- **Customize gameplay: Note size, Stage hint offset and Stage hint height** (kept per noteskin, 4K and 7K apart). Note size draws the notes, hold heads, bodies and ends bigger or smaller than their column. Stage hint offset moves the skin's hit line (and osu!'s judgement line) up or down from the hit position, and Stage hint height makes it taller or thinner; for skins without receptors, whose only hit marker is the stage hint.
- **The stage hint is one picture across the whole stage**, as osu! and Quaver draw it. It used to be drawn once per column, so skins with gaps between columns or columns of different widths (R Skin - AZUSA soo-, for one) showed a broken line with brighter overlaps.
