- **A new launcher.** Play, Games, What's new and Options, in VSRG Hub's violet and pink. It runs from anywhere: the first time, it puts itself where you choose (with a desktop and Start menu shortcut), finds Etterna (or asks where it is, with a link to get it), and installs VSRG Hub into Etterna with one click. From then on it updates VSRG Hub, and itself, when a new version is out.

- **What's new**: every version's notes, from TriWheel's first ones to this, newest first.
- **Options before playing**: resolution, fullscreen / borderless / windowed, refresh rate, VSync, frame limits (menus and gameplay), Direct3D or OpenGL, and Etterna's FPS counter.
- **Fixed: song select stuttered and froze when scrolling fast through many songs** (a long spin of the mouse wheel), in every look. Each song panel asked Etterna for its charts' key count in a way that unpacks every chart's notes, again on every step of the list; the song's background picture was also loaded for every song passed. Now the key count comes from the chart's type, the background waits until the list stops, and panels skip redrawing what hasn't changed.
- **Song select: the difficulties scroll like the songs.** The picked difficulty sits in the middle of the list and the mouse wheel goes through a song's difficulties (gliding), then on to the next or previous song; a quick spin of the wheel keeps going through the songs. Rows that pass the play button keep their MSD clear of it.
- **The osu! look's difficulty rows use your skin's song panel art** (its top and bottom, not squashed), the picked one bright and the others dimmer, with the skin's text colours; skins without panel art keep the blue and white rows.
- **Only 4K and 7K charts**: Etterna's 8K (dance-double), 6K and 3K charts are left out of the difficulty lists, and songs that only have those are left out of the song list (VSRG Hub has no notes or skins for them).
