- **F3 / F4 change the scroll speed while playing** (the osu!, osu!lazer and Quaver looks), as in osu! and Quaver: in the song's first seconds (the first 5 seconds, or until just before the first note when the intro is longer). A press is one step; held, it keeps going, faster the longer it's held. The new speed shows at the top of the screen. The keys can be changed in Keybinds.
- **Scroll speed in your game's numbers**: Etterna's C speed is shown and changed as osu!'s scroll speed in the osu! look (27 = C886), osu!lazer's in the lazer look (with its time on screen) and Quaver's in the Quaver look (29.9 = C877), worked out from how fast each game moves its notes. Options > Scroll speed steps by one of them, and takes them typed (27, 30.2) as well as C700, M650 or 2.5x. In osu!lazer and Quaver, Ctrl with F3 / F4 steps by a tenth.
