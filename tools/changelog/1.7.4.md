2026-10-11
- Quaver skins look the way they do in Quaver:
  - Skins downloaded from the Steam Workshop are now available as noteskins too, not only the ones in Quaver's Skins folder.
  - Notes coloured by beat snap, from separate pictures or from a sprite sheet (before, skins that use a sheet were left out).
  - Notes and receptors turned per column, the gap between columns (NotePadding) and note height (WidthForNoteHeightScale).
  - Settings a skin leaves out come from its DefaultSkin (Bar, Arrow or Circle), as in Quaver.
  - Hold ends are centred on the hold's end with the body under them, so long notes no longer show a gap.
  - Receptors are drawn in front of the notes when the skin asks for it.
  - Hit lighting, hold lighting and column lighting.
- Quaver look: playing with a Quaver skin's notes, the judgements, score and combo digits and health bar come from that skin, at its sizes.
- osu! skins: columns of different widths and the gaps between columns (ColumnWidth / ColumnSpacing lists) are kept, and the LightingN / LightingL lighting is drawn over the hit line.
