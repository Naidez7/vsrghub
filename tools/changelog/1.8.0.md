**VSRG Hub 1.8.0**, the first release under its new name (TriWheel before). Everything 1.7.12 to 1.7.32 brought, in one place:

- **Three looks**: osu! stable, osu!lazer and Quaver, each with its own song select, results, main menu and options, drawn with your own skins. Switch from the options or the launcher.
- **VSRG Hub's own rankings**: every chart ranked, 4K and 7K apart, rated with Etterna's calculator. Plays are verified from their replays. The launcher's Online page has rankings (overall, by pattern, this month), profiles with a rating chart, latest plays, plays of the day, best plays and a daily challenge; the same on the web (naidez7.github.io/vsrghub). Failed daily plays go on the day's board, marked.
- **Ratings**: 4K as Etterna's player rating (the mean of the patterns); 7K the aggregate of your best plays' overall ratings, since Etterna's 7K calculator sits far under the plays. Ties on rating go by accuracy.
- **Your osu! and Quaver plays count**: the launcher reads your osu! stable, lazer and Quaver replays, judges them as Etterna would and the game rates them; new plays are picked up on their own at each start, and every rated play becomes a local score in Etterna too, with its replay.
- **Song select**: your osu!, Quaver and Qwilight songs on the wheel with thumbnails; the scoreboard in three scopes (Local, VSRG Hub, EtternaOnline), sortable by rating, accuracy, combo or date, scrolled with the wheel; a click on a VSRG Hub score opens its card; the daily challenge jumps to its chart; song select opens on the song the menu was playing.
- **Results**: the hit offsets plot with a judge switcher and column filter, VSRG Hub's verdict on the play, Watch replay.
- **The launcher**: Play with the look to start in, Games with each game's icon, What's new, Online, Options (Discord presence, keep the launcher open, ranking notices, menu background), Report a problem (a zip plus a GitHub issue), updates from GitHub, and a web link to your profile.
- **Ranking notices**: who passed you and who took a #1 from you, in the launcher, as toasts and in the game (can be turned off).
- **Discord**: what you're playing, in the game and from the launcher.
