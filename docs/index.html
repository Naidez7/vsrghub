All your rhythm games' songs, one place.

VSRG Hub plays your osu!mania, Quaver and Qwilight songs and skins in Etterna, with the look of the game you know, and online rankings where every chart is ranked.

Download for Windows Rankings

Free. Windows 10 and 11. Needs Etterna (free, from etternaonline.com).

What it does

Everything stays where it is: VSRG Hub reads your games' folders and builds Etterna's library and skins from them.

♪Your songs, in Etterna

osu!mania 4K and 7K, Quaver and Qwilight charts, rated with Etterna's MSD by pattern: stream, jumpstream, handstream, stamina, jack, chordjack, tech.

✦Your skins

Notes, judgements, cursor and interface from your osu! and Quaver skins, laid out as in the game they come from.

◐The look you know

Play in osu! stable's look, osu!lazer's or Quaver's: main menu, song select, mods and results.

#Every chart ranked

Make a VSRG Hub account and every play counts: your rating is worked out as Etterna does, 4K and 7K apart, overall and by pattern.

✓Checked scores

Each score is sent with its replay, and the server works its accuracy out again before it counts.

↻Always up to date

The launcher updates VSRG Hub and itself when a new version is out.

Install

  1. Install Etterna if you don't have it (etternaonline.com).
  2. Download VSRG Hub above, unzip it and open VSRG Hub Setup.exe. It asks where to put itself, finds Etterna and your games, and installs.
  3. Press Play. To be on the rankings, make your account on the launcher's Online page.
Windows may warn about a new program (SmartScreen: More info → Run anyway) or Windows Defender may flag it: VSRG Hub isn't signed yet. It's open source; you can read every line on GitHub.
